# PrivEat Terms Of Service Draft

This draft must be reviewed before publication.

PrivEat provides food safety, freshness, leftover, allergen, and nutrition suitability guidance for informational use.

## No Medical Diagnosis

PrivEat does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure disease. Users should consult a qualified clinician or dietitian before making medical decisions.

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for deciding whether food is safe to consume. PrivEat scores are estimates based on user input, local rules, and optional AI analysis.

## Account Deletion

Users may delete local app data and, when backend auth is enabled, request backend account deletion.

## Cloud AI

Cloud AI features may be rate-limited or unavailable. PrivEat may fall back to local analysis when cloud AI is unavailable.
