# NutriSafe Terms Of Service Draft

This draft must be reviewed before publication.

NutriSafe provides food safety, freshness, leftover, allergen, and nutrition suitability guidance for informational use.

## No Medical Diagnosis

NutriSafe does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure disease. Users should consult a qualified clinician or dietitian before making medical decisions.

## User Responsibility

Users are responsible for deciding whether food is safe to consume. NutriSafe scores are estimates based on user input, local rules, and optional AI analysis.

## Account Deletion

Users may delete all local app data through the Delete Account flow.

## Gemini AI

Gemini features may be rate-limited or unavailable. NutriSafe falls back to local analysis when Gemini is unavailable.
